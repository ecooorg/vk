About us

ECO NGO is a non-profit scientific organization dedicated to advancing the open exchange of knowledge and technology worldwide. Driven entirely by non-commercial values, we champion a global community where ideas are shared freely, and we collaborate only with partners who share our commitment to strictly non-profit endeavors.

Our work spans six areas: energy and environment, materials and catalysis, space systems, robotics, AI safety and governance, and long-term strategy and resilience. Our results are published openly, with links to the full texts.

Principles

Non-commercial. Our work is driven entirely by non-commercial values.

Open exchange. Ideas and results are shared freely; our publications are open, with links to the full texts.

Like-minded partners. We collaborate only with partners who share our commitment to strictly non-profit endeavors.

Areas of work

Space Systems

Autonomous constellations, optical links and thermal management.

Robotics

Joints and hydraulic solutions for legged and adaptive machines.

AI Safety & Governance

Architectures and charters for verifiable, bounded oversight of AI systems.

Strategy & Resilience

Frameworks for stability and decision-making under uncertainty.

Energy & Environment

Clean fuels, flameless heating and recovery of valuable metals.

Materials & Catalysis

Rhenium catalysts, protective coatings, aerogels and self-sealing cryogenic systems.
