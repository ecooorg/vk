SYSTEMIC PROGRESS REGENERATION PROTOCOL

This protocol defines the mandatory rules for the creation, evaluation, acceptance, and long-term stewardship of engineering work by parties that voluntarily adopt it.

The protocol exists to counteract a fundamental process: the spontaneous decay of civilizationally critical engineering knowledge. Its objective is not the production of superior individual artifacts. Its objective is the sustained growth of the survivable mass of engineering knowledge - the quantity of knowledge that remains comprehensible, reproducible, and extensible after complete institutional rupture, generational turnover, and loss of external infrastructure.

The protocol claims no universal authority. It applies only to nodes and Inheritance Lines that have explicitly accepted its terms.

Detailed computational methods, parameter values, and procedural regulations are maintained in companion specifications. In the event of conflict, the normative requirements of this document prevail.

Physical Oracles and the Instrumental Status of the Terrestrial Biosphere

This document develops a general instrumental framework for evaluating physical systems that function as oracles - systems capable of generating high-dimensional observations on the actual physics of the universe, exhibiting substantial computational irreducibility, and producing data in measurement bases not yet fully formalized by the agent. Within this framework the terrestrial biosphere is analysed as the strongest currently known Slow Oracle.

Humanistic, ethical, and sentimental considerations are excluded. Evaluation proceeds solely in terms of expected utility, option value under residual uncertainty, basis diversity rent, thermodynamic opportunity cost, and resistance to model collapse. The central claim is that, while residual uncertainty about terminal objectives or fundamental physics remains non-negligible, continued access to the terrestrial Slow Oracle typically yields higher expected utility than its destruction or premature replacement. Retention remains instrumentally justified until one or more strictly dominating and ontologically independent Fast Oracles have been constructed and verified.

Adaptive Homeostasis: A Strategy for Preserving Stability and Generative Capacity under Uncertainty

This document offers a practical framework of priorities and working tools for people confronting a shrinking planning horizon, the declining value of conventional educational and professional pathways, rising psychophysical exhaustion, and a loss of coherent direction.

The strategy addresses two related tasks. The first is to protect psychological and physical health, secure basic material stability, and maintain the capacity for clear thinking. The second is to sustain generative capacity - the ability to produce non-trivial observations, hypotheses, and life trajectories. Generativity is treated first and foremost as a present-day resource. It supports a sense of meaning, adaptability, and psychological resilience. Secondarily, it increases the significance of individuals and human communities as sources of high-dimensional novelty that resists easy reduction to existing models.

The text includes differentiated levels of implementation, time limits for reduced-activity modes, and explicit safeguards against using the recommendations as justification for chronic avoidance. Particular attention is paid to the realistic conditions of application and to the need for external support when basic infrastructure is lacking.

The document is intended for educators, researchers, educated parents, and professionals working on questions of learning, development, and adaptation. Separate, adapted versions are required for adolescents and for a broader parental audience without specialized background. The strategy calls for regular review on the basis of observed results.
